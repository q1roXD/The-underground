# The Underground

A first-person 3D horror game that runs in your browser. You are trapped in a flooded sewer maze with a monster that hunts by sound.

## ▶ Play

**https://q1roXD.github.io/The-underground/The Undergroundtest.html**
**https://q1roXD.github.io/The-underground/UPD0.6v.html**

Works on PC, phones and tablets. No install needed. Headphones recommended.

## Goal

Collect 50 souls on each level to move to a bigger, darker one. Stay quiet, stay in the dark, and don't get caught.

## Controls

**PC**
| Key | Action |
|---|---|
| W A S D | Move |
| Mouse | Look |
| Shift | Sprint (limited stamina) |
| 1 / 2 or E | Switch flashlight / compass |
| Esc | Pause |

**Mobile / tablet**
- Left side: joystick to move
- Right side: drag to look
- Buttons: sprint, flashlight, compass, pause

## Tips

- Everything you do makes noise: sprinting, the flashlight, picking up souls and the compass beeping.
- Watch the NOISE bar. When it is full, the monster comes for you.
- The compass shows the distance to the nearest soul. The closer you are, the faster it beeps.
- Screamers hang in the dark. Don't shine a light in their faces.
- Avoid spikes and red alarm plates.
